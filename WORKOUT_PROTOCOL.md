## 📈 Progression & Overload System

1. **Double Progression Model**:
   - Each exercise has a target rep bracket (e.g. `8 – 10 reps` or `10 – 12 reps`).
   - Keep the weight fixed until you can hit the **top rep number on all sets** with clean form.
   - Once completed, increase weight by **+1.25 kg to +2.5 kg** and repeat.
2. **Rest Period Discipline**:
   - Do not rush or linger between sets. Follow the exact timer (60s–120s) in the app to standardize intensity and metabolic stress.
3. **Execution Quality Over Ego**:
   - Every rep must have a 2-3 second controlled eccentric (lowering) phase. No momentum, swinging, or half reps.
