## Monday — Pull A + Abs

**Focus:** Lat width, biceps, and direct abdominal work.

| # | Exercise (MuscleWiki) | Primary Muscles Trained | Sets × Reps | Rest | Key Cue |
|---:|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| 1 | [Neutral/standard lat pulldown](https://musclewiki.com/exercises/lats/machine) | Latissimus dorsi (lats); biceps assist | 3 × 8–12 | 90–120 sec | Start with a controlled overhead stretch; pull elbows down without swinging your torso. |
| 2 | [Chest-supported T-bar row](https://musclewiki.com/exercise/machine-chest-supported-t-bar-row) | Mid-back, rhomboids, trapezius and lats; rear delts assist | 3 × 8–12 | 120 sec | Keep your chest against the pad; pull smoothly and let the shoulder blades move at the bottom. |
| 3 | [Single-arm cable pulldown](https://musclewiki.com/exercises/lats) | Lats; biceps assist | 2 × 10–15 each arm | 75–90 sec | Keep your trunk steady and drive the elbow toward your hip. |
| 4 | [Incline dumbbell curl](https://musclewiki.com/exercises/biceps) | Biceps brachii, trained from a lengthened position | 3 × 8–12 | 75–90 sec | Keep upper arms relatively still; lower until the elbows are comfortably extended. |
| 5 | [Cable biceps curl](https://musclewiki.com/exercises/biceps) | Biceps brachii; brachialis assists | 2 × 10–15 | 75 sec | Keep elbows steady and avoid leaning back to move the weight. |
| 6 | [Cable rope kneeling crunch](https://musclewiki.com/exercise/cable-rope-kneeling-crunch) | Rectus abdominis (six-pack muscle) | 3 × 10–15 | 60 sec | Curl ribs toward pelvis; don't turn it into a hip hinge. |
| 7 | [Reverse crunch](https://musclewiki.com/exercise/reverse-crunch) | Rectus abdominis; hip flexors assist | 2 × 10–20 | 60 sec | Curl the pelvis off the mat without swinging your legs. |

## Tuesday — Push A

**Focus:** Upper chest, shoulder width, and triceps.

| # | Exercise (MuscleWiki) | Primary Muscles Trained | Sets × Reps | Rest | Key Cue |
|---:|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| 1 | [Incline dumbbell bench press](https://musclewiki.com/exercise/dumbbell-incline-bench-press) | Upper chest (clavicular pectoralis major); anterior delts and triceps assist | 4 × 6–10 | 2–3 min | Set the bench around 20–30°; lower the dumbbells with control and use a comfortable range. |
| 2 | [Dumbbell shoulder press](https://musclewiki.com/exercises/shoulders) | Anterior deltoids; lateral delts and triceps assist | 3 × 8–12 | 2 min | Keep your ribs down and avoid forcing a painful depth. |
| 3 | [Cable single-arm low lateral raise](https://musclewiki.com/exercise/cable-low-single-arm-lateral-raise) | Lateral deltoid (side shoulder) | 3 × 12–20 each arm | 60–90 sec | Raise out to the side without shrugging or swinging. |
| 4 | [Machine pec fly](https://musclewiki.com/exercise/machine-pec-fly) | Pectoralis major (chest) | 2 × 10–15 | 75–90 sec | Use a controlled stretch; bring the arms together without slamming the handles. |
| 5 | [Cable rope pushdown](https://musclewiki.com/exercises/triceps) | Triceps brachii, especially lateral and medial heads | 3 × 10–15 | 60–90 sec | Keep upper arms near your sides and extend your elbows under control. |

## Wednesday — Legs + Abs

**Focus:** Balanced leg development and a second abdominal session.

| # | Exercise (MuscleWiki) | Primary Muscles Trained | Sets × Reps | Rest | Key Cue |
|---:|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| 1 | [Machine hack squat](https://musclewiki.com/exercise/machine-hack-squat) | Quadriceps; gluteus maximus assists | 3 × 8–12 | 2–3 min | Keep your back against the pad; descend only as far as you can control comfortably. |
| 2 | [Leg extension](https://musclewiki.com/exercises/quads) | Quadriceps | 2 × 12–15 | 75–90 sec | Lift smoothly, briefly squeeze the quads, and lower under control. |
| 3 | [Machine seated leg curl](https://musclewiki.com/exercise/machine-seated-leg-curl) | Hamstrings | 3 × 10–15 | 90 sec | Align the knee joint with the machine pivot; curl without lifting your hips. |
| 4 | [Dumbbell Romanian deadlift](https://musclewiki.com/exercise/dumbbell-romanian-deadlift) | Hamstrings and gluteus maximus; spinal erectors stabilize | 3 × 8–12 | 2 min | Hinge at the hips with a stable back; stop at a comfortable hamstring stretch. Skip if it aggravates back discomfort. |
| 5 | [Machine seated calf raise](https://musclewiki.com/exercise/machine-seated-calf-raises) | Soleus primarily; gastrocnemius assists | 3 × 12–20 | 60–90 sec | Use a comfortable bottom stretch and pause briefly at the top. |
| 6 | [Cable rope kneeling crunch](https://musclewiki.com/exercise/cable-rope-kneeling-crunch) | Rectus abdominis | 2 × 10–15 | 60 sec | Curl your torso rather than pulling with your arms. |
| 7 | [Reverse crunch](https://musclewiki.com/exercise/reverse-crunch) | Rectus abdominis; hip flexors assist | 2 × 10–20 | 60 sec | Lift your pelvis with control; avoid momentum. |

## Thursday — Pull B + Biceps

**Focus:** Back thickness and direct biceps work.

| # | Exercise (MuscleWiki) | Primary Muscles Trained | Sets × Reps | Rest | Key Cue |
|---:|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| 1 | [Lat pulldown](https://musclewiki.com/exercises/lats) | Latissimus dorsi; biceps assist | 3 × 8–12 | 90–120 sec | Pull your elbows down; avoid rocking backward to finish reps. |
| 2 | [Chest-supported T-bar row](https://musclewiki.com/exercise/machine-chest-supported-t-bar-row) | Mid-back, rhomboids, trapezius and lats | 3 × 8–12 | 120 sec | Keep chest contact with the pad and avoid jerking the weight. |
| 3 | [Reverse pec deck](https://musclewiki.com/exercises/shoulders) | Rear deltoids; upper-back muscles assist | 2 × 12–20 | 75 sec | Keep shoulders down and move from the shoulder joint. |
| 4 | [EZ-bar curl](https://musclewiki.com/exercises/biceps) | Biceps brachii; brachialis assists | 3 × 8–12 | 90 sec | Keep wrists comfortable, elbows steady, and torso quiet. |
| 5 | [Dumbbell hammer curl](https://musclewiki.com/exercises/biceps) | Brachialis and brachioradialis; biceps also contribute | 2 × 10–15 | 75 sec | Maintain a neutral grip and avoid swinging. |

## Friday — Push B

**Focus:** Chest development, shoulder caps, rear delts and triceps.

| # | Exercise (MuscleWiki) | Primary Muscles Trained | Sets × Reps | Rest | Key Cue |
|---:|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| 1 | [Incline dumbbell bench press](https://musclewiki.com/exercise/dumbbell-incline-bench-press) | Upper chest; anterior delts and triceps assist | 3 × 8–12 | 2–3 min | Use a 20–30° incline and a consistent range of motion. |
| 2 | [Machine chest press](https://musclewiki.com/exercise/machine-chest-press) | Pectoralis major; triceps and anterior delts assist | 3 × 8–12 | 2 min | Adjust the seat so handles begin around mid-chest; don't force the shoulders backward. |
| 3 | [Cable single-arm low lateral raise](https://musclewiki.com/exercise/cable-low-single-arm-lateral-raise) | Lateral deltoid | 3 × 12–20 each arm | 60–90 sec | Use a controlled arc and avoid momentum. |
| 4 | [Machine pec fly](https://musclewiki.com/exercise/machine-pec-fly) | Pectoralis major | 2 × 12–15 | 75–90 sec | Maintain a slight elbow bend and a comfortable stretch. |
| 5 | [Single-arm overhead cable triceps extension](https://musclewiki.com/exercise/cable-single-arm-overhead-tricep-extension) | Triceps brachii, with the long head trained at a longer shoulder position | 3 × 10–15 | 60–90 sec | Keep upper arm pointed upward and extend the elbow without arching your back. |

## Saturday — V-Taper + Arms + Abs

**Focus:** Lateral delts, lats, one more biceps stimulus and abdominal work.

| # | Exercise (MuscleWiki) | Primary Muscles Trained | Sets × Reps | Rest | Key Cue |
|---:|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| 1 | [Dumbbell lateral raise](https://musclewiki.com/exercises/shoulders) | Lateral deltoids | 3 × 12–20 | 75 sec | Raise to a comfortable height without shrugging or swinging. |
| 2 | [Straight-arm cable pulldown](https://musclewiki.com/exercises/lats) | Latissimus dorsi; teres major assists | 3 × 12–15 | 75–90 sec | Keep elbows slightly bent and bring the bar toward your thighs without turning it into a triceps press. |
| 3 | [Cable bar face pull](https://musclewiki.com/exercise/cable-bar-face-pull) | Rear deltoids, middle trapezius and external-rotation muscles | 2 × 15–20 | 75 sec | Pull toward face level with elbows out; keep the movement controlled. |
| 4 | [Cable biceps curl](https://musclewiki.com/exercises/biceps) | Biceps brachii; brachialis assists | 3 × 10–15 | 75 sec | Keep upper arms stable and control the return. |
| 5 | [Cable rope kneeling crunch](https://musclewiki.com/exercise/cable-rope-kneeling-crunch) | Rectus abdominis | 2 × 10–15 | 60 sec | Exhale as you curl ribs toward pelvis; do not yank the rope with your arms. |
| 6 | [Reverse crunch](https://musclewiki.com/exercise/reverse-crunch) | Rectus abdominis; hip flexors assist | 2 × 10–20 | 60 sec | Roll your pelvis upward slowly and lower without swinging. |

## Sunday — Rest and Recovery

- Optional 20–30 minute easy walk or gentle mobility work.
- Maintain your usual hydration (3,500 ml target, daytime reminders 10 AM – 10 PM).
- Aim for 7.5–9 hours of sleep.
- Continue your daily **5g Creatine Monohydrate** at 8:00 AM and **1 capsule Vitamin B Complex** at 10:00 PM.
